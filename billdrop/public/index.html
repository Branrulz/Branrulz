      The protocol

      Every bill is vetted

      1. A member submits a bill with their statement.
      2. Our reviewers verify it with the biller before it goes live.
      3. A patron settles it — in part or in full.
      4. Funds go directly to the biller. Never to an individual.
      5. Patrons never see a member's statement, contact details or last name, and can't message them.

      *Everyone is invited.
